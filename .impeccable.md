# QuantTrade design context

User-provided context: QuantTrade is a personal trading terminal for an active manual and agent-assisted trader using Lighter and Hyperliquid. Core jobs are finding opportunities, comparing the same native market across venues, reviewing exact trade legs and managing orders without switching to multiple interfaces.

The user explicitly requests a smaller, cleaner, tighter experience. Their scanner references use compact rows with aligned rates, clear venue/direction labels, dense expanded tables, secondary chart/report views and a concise two-leg arbitrage review. Preserve QuantTrade's existing dark emerald palette, typography and venue logos. Voice: precise, restrained, decisive. Show the data and primary action first; move methodology into disclosure. Avoid decorative metrics, fabricated signals, unsupported venues and claims of atomic cross-exchange execution.

Desktop expands into a bounded, scrollable report; mobile must keep controls and review actions reachable without page-wide overflow. Funding projections, settled history and unavailable data must remain distinct.

Explicit user correction: scanner reports and opportunity reviews should open as centered floating cards with tabs and a backdrop, not a persistent sidebar or a page-width takeover. Preserve the discovery cards behind the dialog. Use a compact review dialog and a wider report dialog, with one active modal surface at a time and a clear return path from opportunity review to its report.
